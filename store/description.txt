Zeeba Health helps you turn symptoms that keep getting brushed off into clear, dated evidence you can take to your doctor.

Pelvic pain, heavy or irregular periods, perimenopause, exhaustion, bowel or bladder problems: if you have been told it is "just stress", Zeeba Health helps you show what has really been happening, in numbers and in your own words.

RECORD
• Say or type what happened, the way you would tell a friend. Every note is saved with its date.
• Add how strong it was from 0 to 10, where you felt it, and what it stopped you doing.
• Log bleeding and spotting days in a couple of taps.

PROVE
• Evidence turns your notes into counts and charts: how many days, how bad, and how often it got in the way.
• A calendar and a week-by-week view make the pattern easy to see.

REACH
• For my doctor builds one clear summary for your appointment: how often and how strong, periods logged, what it got in the way of, what you have tried, and your own words.
• Save it as a PDF, print it, or show it on your screen during the appointment.

MADE FOR YOU
• Light and dark mode, Dynamic Type and VoiceOver support.
• Wren, a knitted hot-water bottle, keeps you company on the hard days.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your notes are private to your account. We do not sell your information or show you ads.
• Delete your account and everything you have saved from inside the app, at any time.

Zeeba Health is a record-keeping tool. It does not diagnose, give medical advice or replace a healthcare professional. If you feel very unwell, contact your GP, NHS 111 or 999, or your local emergency number.
